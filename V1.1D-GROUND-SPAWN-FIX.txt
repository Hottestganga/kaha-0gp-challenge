0GP Race v1.1d - Ground Spawn First-Pickup Fix

Fix:
- Natural ground items that are already loaded before a race starts are now seeded into the eligible world-spawn cache.
- Previously, the plugin depended on RuneLite's ItemSpawned event, so an item already visible before the race began could fail to score on the first pickup.
- Normal ItemSpawned handling remains unchanged for items that appear after the race starts.

Regression test:
1. Stand near a known natural map spawn and wait until the item is visible.
2. Start a fresh race while that item is already on the ground.
3. Pick it up immediately. It should score on the first pickup.
4. Wait for it to respawn and pick it up again. It should also score.
5. Verify an NPC drop still scores.
6. Verify bank import/refund and multiplayer sync still behave as before.
