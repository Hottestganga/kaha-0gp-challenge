# Changelog

All notable changes to **0GP Race** will be documented in this file.

The project follows a milestone-based version history while in Alpha.

---

# v1.1e – Alpha
Released: 7 August 2026

## Added
- 30-second over-budget grace period before disqualification.
- Automatic cancellation of grace period when score returns to zero or above.
- Improved race fairness for long competitions.
- Activity log entries for over-budget, restored balance and disqualification events.

## Improved
- Prevents accidental disqualification from misclick bank withdrawals.
- Cleaner handling of imported item withdrawals.

---

# v1.1d – Alpha

- Natural ground item detection now supports items already spawned before a race begins.
- Improved world spawn tracking.

## Fixed
- Fixed first pickup of natural ground spawns not counting towards race GP.
