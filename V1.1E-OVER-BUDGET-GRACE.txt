0GP Race v1.1e - 30 Second Over-Budget Grace

What changed
- Going below 0 GP no longer instantly disqualifies the player.
- A 30-second wall-clock grace period starts the first time the score becomes negative.
- The sidebar status shows the negative balance and seconds until DQ.
- Returning enough imported value to reach 0 GP or higher cancels the countdown immediately.
- Going more negative does NOT restart the 30-second timer.
- Logging out does NOT pause the over-budget countdown (prevents abuse).
- If the grace period expires while still negative, the run is disqualified.
- Activity log records OVER BUDGET, BUDGET RESTORED, and DISQUALIFIED events.
- If race time reaches zero while the player is still negative, the run is disqualified.

Test
1. Start at 0 GP.
2. Withdraw an old item so score becomes negative.
3. Confirm OVER BUDGET and a countdown near 30 seconds appears.
4. Deposit the imported item back before 30 seconds. Confirm race continues and event log says BUDGET RESTORED.
5. Repeat and leave the score negative for >30 seconds. Confirm DQ.
6. Repeat, go negative, log out, wait >30 seconds. Confirm the grace timer did not pause.
