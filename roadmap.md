# 0GP Race Roadmap

This roadmap outlines the planned direction for the 0GP Race project.

---

# Version 1.2 – Beta

## Additional Activity Support

The goal is to support every legitimate method of obtaining wealth in Old School RuneScape.

Planned additions include:

- Barrows reward chest
- Chambers of Xeric rewards
- Theatre of Blood rewards
- Tombs of Amascut rewards
- Wintertodt supply crate
- Tempoross reward pool
- Guardians of the Rift rewards
- Clue scroll caskets
- Crystal Chest
- Brimstone Chest
- Larran's Chest
- Rogue's Chests
- Thieving stalls
- Thieving chests
- Wall safes

---

# Version 1.3

## Race Improvements

- Team races

- Improved race statistics
- Better activity history
- Race export
- Import/export settings

# Long Term Vision

Our goal is to make 0GP Race the premier GP racing platform for the Old School RuneScape community.

The project will continue expanding while remaining simple to install through the RuneLite Plugin Hub.
